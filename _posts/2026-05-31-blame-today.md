i have had this little app idea bouncing around in my head for probably 15 or 20 years. i don't know why i never made it before, but with ai helpers now, it's impossible to not make any little apps that you have in your head as a creative excercise. what was it, well let me talk about it.

# blame

so once upon a time, i had this idea for a app to blame people. whoever. it is just a silly idea, what if you could go, have a text field to type in anything you want, and click the button and it would just add up all the votes anyone ever typed in. well now i have made it and that app is https://blame.today.

why blame today, not just blame? well i found that there were already some apps out there called blame and while i was noodling around, i came up with the idea of asking the question 'who do you blame today?' after i found that the domain blame.today was available. i mean, had i found blame.ai or blame.me or who knows what else, maybe i would have used one of those, but this is what i found so i went this route.
